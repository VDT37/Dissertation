# Checkpoint selection, reproduced from the training logs

Generated by `select_checkpoint.py`. Every figure below is read from the `sampled` block of each arm's `train_log.json`; nothing is recomputed and no GPU is involved.

Rule, precommitted before either arm was scored: hard gate on sampled model-mean MAE at or below the advection control on the same crops, then maximise CRPSS = 1 - crps/crps_adv, then break ties within 0.005 CRPSS on member PSD closest to the codec ceiling of 0.903.

CAVEAT. These are 16-crop in-training diagnostics, not full-split scorecards. `docs/Diffusion_MultiLead_Results.md` section 12 measures that this statistic understates member PSD by 15 to 32 percent, so the tiebreak ran on a biased input. This table reproduces what the rule did, not what a better-informed rule would have done.
